Strategy Logic

How every DG strategy decides what to bet — thresholds, stakes, markets, and how they differ.

log_type = main

Main bets

Rule-based DataGaffer scenario buckets. If the sim projection crosses a fixed threshold, take the matching market. No edge/EV filter and no multi-signal stack. Source: DG projected win % and projected goals. Stake: flat 1.0u.

Scenario Trigger Markets logged
Win 60%+ Home/away win % ≥ 60 ML, Win-or-Draw (1X/X2)
Win 70%+ Win % ≥ 70 Same, higher bar
Total 3.5+ Projected total ≥ 3.5 O1.5, O2.5
Total 4.0+ Projected total ≥ 4.0 O1.5, O2.5, O3.5
Team goals 2.0+ Team projected ≥ 2.0 Team O0.5, O1.5
Team goals 2.5+ Team projected ≥ 2.5 Team O0.5, O1.5
BTTS Both teams projected ≥ 1.5 BTTS Yes
Low total ≤ 2.0 Projected total ≤ 2.0 U2.5, U3.5

High volume, historical hit-rate style. The same fixture can create several Main bets (e.g. both O1.5 and O2.5 when total ≥ 3.5).

log_type = arahus (+ v2 / live_v1 variants)

Arahus bets

Custom projection engine that blends DG sims, ratings (DGRtg/ORtg/DRtg), pace/NEC/AGIX, xG/luck, highlights, and book odds — then only picks when signal families agree. Isolated from Main and +EV logs.

Arahus V1 (main Arahus book)

  • Confidence ≥ 62 (sum of weighted signals)
  • If odds exist: edge ≥ 2pp and EV > 0
  • Can pick without odds when ARAHUS_ALLOW_NO_ODDS=true
  • Max 3 picks per fixture
  • Rank by confidence → edge → EV; avoid conflicting families (overs vs unders, duplicate ML)

Stake ladder: base 1.0u · 1.5u if confidence ≥ 78 and edge ≥ 5 · 0.75u if confidence < 68.

Markets: O1.5/O2.5/O3.5, U2.5, BTTS, ML, team O0.5/O1.5, DC 1X/X2, corners O8.5–O10.5. Every evaluated market (picked and skipped) is written to the decision log.

Arahus v2

Frozen forward test: O2.5 only, odds 1.30–1.49, edge ≥ 3pp (live), confidence ≥ 66, EV > 0, flat 1u. O3.5 logged as research only.

Arahus Live V1

Filter on existing V1 picks: keep only O2.5 with odds 1.30–1.49; preserve V1 stake. Auto-sync off by default (ARAHUS_LIVE_V1_ENABLED).

log_type = ev

+EV bets

Price-driven. Only bet when the model says the market is underpriced, then confirm the projection shape supports that market — not “DG scenario crossed a line.” Stake: flat 1.0u.

Must pass all of:

  • Edge ≥ 2 percentage points
  • EV > 0
  • Verdict in STRONG VALUE / VALUE / LEAN
  • xG / model filter for that market, for example:
Market Model filter
Over 2.5 Total xG ≥ 2.85
Over 3.5 Total xG ≥ 3.65
Under 2.5 Total xG ≤ 2.35
Team O1.5 Team xG ≥ 1.85
BTTS Yes Both teams xG ≥ 0.95
Moneyline Team xG edge ≥ 0.25 vs opponent
Draw Draw model ≥ 26% and xG gap ≤ 0.45

Markets come from the fixture dashboard: totals, team goals, BTTS, ML, draw, DC 1X/X2.

log_type = h2h

H2H bets

Mirrors DataGaffer Head-to-Head Trends: only bet markets that have hit often enough in prior meetings between these two teams. Stake: flat 1.0u per qualified market.

  • Min meetings: 6
  • Min historical hit rate: ≥ 75%
Family Markets
Goals O2.5, O3.5, BTTS Yes
Corners O8.5, O9.5, O10.5
Shots on target O7.5, O8.5, O9.5
Win / Draw Home, Draw, Away

Goals + 1X2 + corners priced from Polymarket (corners via {slug}-total-corners); DataGaffer all_odds is the corners fallback. SOT odds stay manual. Settlement for corners/SOT uses DataGaffer daily_accuracy first, then API-Football. Auto PM pricing on refresh is off by default (AUTO_SYNC_H2H_PM_ON_REFRESH).

log_type = lm · UI: Line Movement

LM bets

Form + H2H filter for Over 1.5 goals only. Despite the “Line Movement” label, selection is not live steam — it requires both teams to score enough in form and H2H O1.5 history. Stake: flat 1.0u.

  • Market: Over 1.5 only
  • Home GF ≥ 1.0 and Away GF ≥ 1.0
  • H2H O1.5 % ≥ 80
  • Home form O1.5 % ≥ 80
  • Away form O1.5 % ≥ 80

Data from team_actuals.json + h2h_form_combined.json. Odds from match over_1_5_odds.

log_type = no · UI: No-Vig Model

NO bets

Fade weak sides: when a team’s win % and projected goals are both low, bet the double chance against them (they “not to win”). Stake: flat 1.0u.

  • Qualify when win % < 50 and projected goals < 1.0
  • Oppose home → market X2 (Draw or Away), odds dc_draw_away_odds
  • Oppose away → market 1X (Home or Draw), odds dc_home_draw_odds
  • Logged qualifier: not_win_pct = 100 − win_pct

log_type = cs · UI: Closing Steam

CS bets (Correct Score baskets)

Despite the “Closing Steam” label, this is a dutch correct-score basket on Polymarket: buy DG’s top scorelines so any single hit profits. Stake is shared across legs (default basket 1.0u total).

  • Model: Dixon–Coles Poisson; try basket sizes 5 then 4
  • Candidate pool: top 10 DG scorelines (CS_CANDIDATE_POOL)
  • Min guaranteed ROI if any leg hits: 5% (CS_MIN_GUARANTEED_ROI)
  • Ask buffer +0.005; max leg price 0.60
  • Only fixtures within 72h kickoff (CS_MAX_HOURS_AHEAD)
  • Pick basket with max EV units; near-ties prefer wider size
  • Model +EV not required by default (CS_REQUIRE_POSITIVE_EV=false)

Sync skips fixtures already logged. Auto-sync on refresh defaults on (AUTO_SYNC_CS_ON_REFRESH).

log_type = prop · NBA / MLB props

Prop Model

Phase 1 is stats scrape + normalize only (NBA/MLB). There is no automated pick engine yet — bets are logged manually (stat, line, side, stake, odds). Later phases (lines, projections, edge) are not live.

  • Storage: shared Postgres pm_* tables (or local SQLite fallback)
  • Manual stake default: 1.0u
